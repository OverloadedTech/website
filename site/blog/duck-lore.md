So, there's a duck in the top bar. She's holding a terminal and asking you a question. That question is really the whole post, but people keep emailing me asking what she is, so here's the long version.

## The setup

What you're looking at is a kiosk. You've seen these: the screen at the mall, the ticket machine at the station, the museum thing that's been showing the same slideshow since 2014. A kiosk is a normal computer with one application locked on top of it, so you can only do the one thing.

Sometimes the kiosk glitches. The application dies, and for a second you see what was underneath the whole time: a desktop, a taskbar, someone's wallpaper. That moment is the best thing about kiosks and I've wanted to build one for years.

## The duck

The duck is the one process on this machine that was never supposed to be running, and she's the reason you can get out at all. She hands you a terminal. Nobody told her to.

A few people have already worked out that you can point that terminal at her. I'm not going to tell you what happens. I'll tell you that it doesn't work, it will never work, and you should absolutely try anyway.

She is immortal, and given what she did for you, that seems fair. Be nice to her. Or don't. She has been fine so far.

## What to actually do

Open the terminal. Type `help`. Read it like you would read the help output of a program you just installed: skeptically, and then try the thing that looks like it shouldn't be there. Then type `lore`.

Some things aren't part of the puzzle, so I'll just say them: commands ignore case, they don't care if you put `sudo` in front, and nothing you do here can permanently break anything. There's also a way to wipe yourself back to a clean slate if you want to start over. Everything else, go find it.

<p class="small">If you end up somewhere that looks broken rather than intentional, that's a bug, and I'd like the email.</p>
